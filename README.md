# Coffee Sales Business Analysis using SQL

## Project Overview

This project uses **SQL to answer practical business questions from coffee sales data**.

Rather than focusing only on writing SQL queries, the objective is to approach the dataset from a business analyst's perspective — investigating customers, products, cities, sales performance and potential market opportunities.

The project is being developed continuously as part of my SQL and business analytics practice.

## Business Questions

The analysis currently explores questions such as:

1. How many people in each city could potentially consume coffee if 25% of the population drinks coffee?
2. How much revenue was generated from coffee sales during Q4 2023?
3. Which coffee products sell the most?
4. What is the average sales value across cities and customers?
5. How do city population and customer activity compare?
6. What are the top-selling coffee products within each city?

Additional business questions will be added as the analysis develops.

## Dataset

The database contains four main tables:

* `city`
* `customers`
* `product`
* `sales`

These tables allow analysis across geographical, customer, product and transaction-level dimensions.

## Current Analysis Areas

### Market Potential

Estimate potential coffee consumers within each city using population data.

### Revenue Analysis

Analyse coffee sales revenue by city and reporting period.

### Product Performance

Identify high-performing products based on sales activity.

### Customer Analysis

Analyse average customer spending and identify higher-value customers.

### Geographic Analysis

Compare performance across cities and investigate market potential.
